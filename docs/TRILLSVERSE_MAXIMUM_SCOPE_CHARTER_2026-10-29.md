## Three states — never conflate
1. **Observed / operational:** demonstrated public or physical behavior with dated evidence.
2. **Developing / experimental:** code, study, design or prototypes with a named unverified acceptance condition.
3. **Possibility Reserve:** exploratory concepts, including long-range and speculative mechanisms, without any claim of deployment or current approval.

A current product decision does not erase historical exploration. A former proposal does not imply a current financial product or commitment. Preserve operative safety, financial, legal, consent and participant terms unless separately authorized to amend them.

## Universal Expression / Universal Consequences
An action or change is interesting for its effect on future possible states, not solely its immediate output. Track Current State → Action → Weight Shift → New State → New Possibilities, plus observation and learning. Neither recognition nor a story establishes actual effect without evidence.
