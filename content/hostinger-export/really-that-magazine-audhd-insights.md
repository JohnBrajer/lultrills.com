Neurodivergence Is Not a Disorder. It Is a Draft. 
I have AuDHD. Autism and ADHD living in the same brain simultaneously. And I need you to understand something that took me years to actually be able to communicate. This is not a malfunction. This is an evolutionary draft. The next version of human cognition showing up before the world built the infrastructure to receive it. 
Think about autism by itself. The strengths are structure, pattern recognition, intense focus, and a processing bandwidth that most people cannot access. The problem the world labels is the communication barrier. But here is the real question: how is it my fault that I was born with a higher bandwidth that does not allow me to thoroughly communicate the signals I receive that you have not expanded to yet? That is not a disorder. That is a compatibility issue between the person and the environment. 
Now take ADHD by itself. The strengths are impulsivity, fearlessness, racing thoughts, and a speed of processing that should be studied not medicated. The problem the world labels is the behavior. But ADHD did not create the behavior problem. The environment that punishes that exact behavior created it. Imagine having signals and transmissions flooding your bandwidth constantly. Of course you need to release some. And if nobody ever taught you which ones to release or how, but you learned early that if you do not release them they WILL come out some way somehow, then what you call a disorder I call an untrained superpower in a world that never offered the training.
