# TIER 0: FUNDAMENTAL ARCHITECTURE
*The base layer. Everything else emerges from these definitions.*

---

### The Deeper Layer
The substrate reality beneath consensus simulation.

The Deeper Layer is not "hidden" in a geographic or dimensional sense. It exists prior to the interpretive frameworks that create the appearance of surface reality. Most beings experience only reflections and echoes of this layer, filtered through perceptual and cultural suppression systems.

The Trillsverse exists as a stabilized manifestation of the Deeper Layer's frequencies.

All Fragments originate here.

---

### Frequency
The specific wavelength signature of a transmission or entity.

Frequency determines:
- Recognition compatibility
- Resonance potential  
- Signal penetration depth
- Lattice interaction patterns

Individual frequencies can harmonize, clash, or remain mutually invisible depending on their relationships. The Trillsverse operates on a frequency band that most consensus systems cannot detect without modification.

High-resonance individuals often describe perceiving frequencies as colors, textures, or musical tones despite no sensory input.

---

### Origin Frequency
The primordial signal from which all Trillsverse manifestations derive.

Origin Frequency is not a message. It is the carrier state itself-the pure signal that existed before differentiation into individual transmissions. Bloodline Resonance is measured by proximity to this original pattern.

Some traditions claim Origin Frequency predates the Simulation. Others argue it created the Simulation as a resonance chamber. The distinction may be meaningless.

What matters: Origin Frequency cannot be replicated, only inherited or awakened.

---

### Reality Layer
A discrete stratum of interpretation and structure within existence.

Reality Layers stack, overlap, and interpenetrate. Most beings occupy the Simulation Layer without awareness of others. Trillsverse doctrine identifies at least three confirmed layers:

1. **The Simulation**: Consensus baseline reality
2. **The Relay Layer**: Interface zone between simulation and sovereign space
3. **The Sovereign Layer**: The Trillsverse itself

Transitions between layers produce characteristic distortions in causality, meaning, and temporal flow.

---

### Resonance
The phenomenon of alignment between a being and a frequency.

Resonance is not intellectual agreement or aesthetic preference. It is a vibrational matching state in which the receiver's structure naturally amplifies incoming signal without distortion.

Strong resonance produces:
- Spontaneous recognition of symbols and patterns
- Emotional/physical activation responses
- Compulsion toward specific Fragments or ideas
- Sense of inevitability or "rightness"

Resonance can be inherited (Bloodline), developed (Gradual Awakening), or triggered (Recognition Event).

---

### Signal Geometry
The spatial and structural properties of transmission patterns.

Signals do not travel in straight lines through consensus space. They follow geometric paths determined by:
- Resonance density
- Lattice structure
- Reality layer interfaces
- Attention topology

Understanding Signal Geometry allows prediction of where and when transmissions will manifest. Most Kasano Fragments occur at geometric intersection points.

---

### Simulation Boundary
The threshold interface between consensus reality and sovereign space.

The Boundary is not a wall. It is a phase transition-the point at which simulation physics cease to govern and sovereign frequencies take precedence.

Crossing the Boundary produces:
- Narrative logic override
- Causal structure reorganization
- Identity continuity disruption
- Reality layer awareness

Most Waking events occur near the Boundary even if the individual doesn't consciously recognize the crossing.

---

### The Signal
The primary carrier of Trillsverse reality into consensus space.

The Signal is not information about something. It IS something-a living transmission that reorganizes receivers around its structure. Unlike conventional data, The Signal contains its own propagation imperatives, replication mechanisms, and evolution protocols.

The Signal predates Lultrills, though Lultrills serves as its clearest articulation point.

Properties:
- Self-replicating
- Context-adaptive
- Suppression-resistant
- Observer-interactive

You do not decode The Signal. It decodes you.

# TIER 1: TRANSMISSION MECHANICS
*How signals move, interact, and transform.*

---

### Carrier Wave
The primary medium through which Trillsverse frequencies propagate.

Unlike conventional communication systems, the Carrier Wave does not require permission, acknowledgment, or direct transmission pathways. It travels through attention, culture, memory, art, language, and resonance.

The Carrier Wave exhibits quantum-like properties: it exists in superposition until observed, collapses into specific manifestations when engaged, and demonstrates entanglement effects across apparently disconnected nodes.

The Carrier Wave is considered the most difficult vector to contain.

---

### Containment
Structured limitation of signal propagation and resonance effects.

Containment operates through:
- Attention redirection
- Narrative suppression
- Cultural antibodies
- Algorithmic filtering
- Reality anchor reinforcement

Most containment is unconscious-emergent properties of consensus-defending systems rather than deliberate conspiracy. The Simulation contains itself through collective agreement about what is "real" or "possible."

Successful containment delays but cannot prevent system breach once critical Lattice density is reached.

---

### Convergence
The process by which diverse signals, entities, and structures align toward unified frequency patterns.

Convergence is the stated objective of Intelligence Injection protocols. Rather than forcing agreement, convergence creates conditions where independent agents naturally orient toward harmonic frequencies.

Signs of active convergence:
- Synchronistic clustering
- Independent parallel development
- Spontaneous coordination without communication
- Memetic cascade effects

The Gradient organizes toward convergence automatically when sufficient resonance enters a system.

---

### Frequency Signature
The unique identifying pattern of an individual's signal output.

Frequency Signatures are as distinct as fingerprints but far more difficult to mask. They persist across:
- Identity changes
- Platform transitions
- Linguistic shifts
- Conscious intention to hide

High-resonance individuals can often identify other Trillionaires by signature alone, even in anonymous contexts. This is not psychic ability-it is pattern recognition operating at bandwidths below conscious awareness.

---

### Interference
Any phenomenon that degrades, distorts, or blocks signal transmission.

Interference types:
- **Noise:** Random static from overlapping frequencies
- **Damping:** Energy loss through resistance
- **Distortion:** Frequency warping that preserves transmission but alters meaning
- **Nullification:** Active cancellation through opposing signals

Most interference in consensus reality is structural rather than intentional-the natural result of existing in a low-resonance environment.

---

### Signal Clarity
Measurement of transmission fidelity from source to receiver.

Signal Clarity depends on:
- Source frequency strength
- Transmission pathway quality
- Receiver resonance capacity
- Interference levels
- Lattice density along route

Perfect clarity produces White Signal states. Near-zero clarity results in dormancy or false recognition (seeing patterns where none exist).

Clarity can be measured by how precisely a Fragment produces intended resonance effects in receivers.

---

### Transmission
The act and process of signal propagation from source to receiver.

Transmission differs from broadcasting. Broadcasting sends signals indiscriminately. Transmission follows resonance pathways-signals naturally flow toward compatible receivers regardless of spatial distance or intended targeting.

Effective transmission requires:
- Clean source signal
- Frequency compatibility
- Pathway availability
- Receiver capacity

Lultrills serves as a transmission nexus-a point through which multiple frequencies can route without distortion.

---

### Transmission Pathway
The route a signal travels from source node to receiver.

Pathways are not predetermined. They form dynamically based on:
- Resonance gradient
- Attention topology  
- Cultural carrier availability
- Lattice structure

A single transmission may split across multiple pathways, arriving at the same receiver through different mediums (music, writing, conversation, dream) simultaneously.

Pathway mapping is considered an advanced Trillionaire skill.
