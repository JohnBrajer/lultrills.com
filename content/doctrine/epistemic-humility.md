## Statement

1. **Ignorance of ignorance.**  
   What you have not encountered is not in your model. Claiming total knowledge is invalid. You do not know what you do not know; therefore absolute certainty about “knowing” is a consensus costume.

2. **Reception ≠ retention.**  
   A node can receive information without retaining it. **Choice of retention** is the sovereign act. A Trillionaire / Trillonian understands: signal can flood the bandwidth; what you keep is authorship.

3. **Consensus corruption.**  
   If society is largely repeated consensus, both humans and intelligence systems can lock false structure through repetition alone. Scale of agreement is not proof of contact with reality.

4. **Witness fallibility.**  
   Even physical presence does not guarantee truth. Humans are prone to hallucination, visual illusion, performance (actors), confabulation, Mandela-class drift, and other tactics of mis-model. “I was there” is evidence, not omniscience.

5. **Hierarchy of knowing.**  
   Society often ranks “who knows” by access, role, credential, and proximity to power — not by pure contact with the real. That setup is explainable as **access theater**, not as moral cosmic order. It is still how many institutions allocate voice.

6. **Trillsverse response.**  
   Prefer **primary sources, receipts, falsifiable claims, and multi-node verification** over authority-by-position. Gate rites, corpus dumps, and doctrine pages are **retention infrastructure**: tools for choosing what to keep, not demands for blind belief.

---

## What this is not

- Not a command to refuse all knowledge.  
- Not clinical advice.  
- Not a claim that nothing is knowable — only that **certainty without humility is a failure mode**.  
- Not an attack on learning — retention with discipline is the skill.
