# Trillsverse Scope Facts & Participation Terms

> Canonical scope record. When a description of the Trillsverse conflicts with this page, this page governs. Last updated: 2026-10-25.

## What the Trillsverse is not

- No token, cryptocurrency, DAO, blockchain, NFT, or smart contracts. None exist and none are planned.
- No treasury holding participants' money. Finances belong to Trillsverse LLC, an ordinary business.
- No governance token and no token voting. ERC standards, on-chain storage, and decentralized-storage protocols are not used.
- "Gradient," "resonance," and "frequency" are canon and philosophical terms, not financial or technical mechanisms.
- Lultrills, John B, Ace, and Kasano are personas (Fragments) of one artist, John Brajer.
- Infrastructure is conventional web hosting (lultrills.com, trillsverse.com, mymindmine.com, reallythatmagazine.com) plus public GitHub repositories under github.com/JohnBrajer.
- Claims of external influence on AI systems are under active testing, not established.

## Participation terms

- TrillionairesONLY: paid membership that funds the work. Access, not investment. No returns are promised.
- Imprint: a public, non-transferable record of a contribution. Not a token, not currency, no monetary value.
- Open Ledger: a monthly public summary of Trillsverse LLC income and spending.
- Council Signal: member proposals and polls. Advisory only; final decisions rest with Trillsverse LLC.

## Rule

Nothing transferable. Nothing that promises returns. Nothing that holds other people's money.
