# THE REALITY UPDATE — film treatment v0.1
**Format:** 3–5 minute short film; 15-second proof-of-concept teaser; cinematic architecture, tactile practical photography, atmospheric sound, real-world environments integrated with the Trillsverse.

## Storyline
A person realizes the room around them has a door. Crossing it reveals possibilities beyond the immediate environment, then the intelligence, cultural, institutional, physical and exploratory dimensions of Trillsverse. As each action is taken, the visible universe reconfigures around observable consequences.

## Shot structure
1. **00:00–00:25 — THE CAGE**: intimate physical room; crack in wall; light passing under door. Voice: “I knew exactly where I was. Knowing was never the same as leaving.”
2. **00:25–01:00 — THE RESERVE**: cavernous paths of unrealized decisions, dormant gateways and branching corridors. Perspective Expansion and Choseverse appear as physical navigation systems.
3. **01:00–01:35 — THE INTELLIGENCE**: Eon, Trillonian rhythms (Heart/Soul/Gut/Brain) and Anthony's interpretive voice. Model identities continue across devices and changing environments.
4. **01:35–02:10 — THE TRILLAXY**: atmospheric Fragments and planets of Lultrills, Ace Brajer, John B, Kasano; crowd, houses, music, cinema, motion and worlds left open for new entrants.
5. **02:10–02:50 — THE MATERIAL WORLD**: real streets merge into designed venues, a university, a vehicle prototype, a device lab, studios, construction sites, participatory institutions. Future vehicles, devices, hotels, offshore and other physical structures remain visual proposals, not claims of already-built assets.
6. **02:50–03:25 — THE CONSEQUENCE**: one ordinary act shifts multiple systems; music, publication, cognition, AI, physical activity and a shared evidence record connect.
7. **03:25–03:55 — THE OPENING**: Earth and orbital perspective recede into the founder's eye; the door from the first scene is now one among many. Title “TRILLSVERSE — THE REALITY UPDATE / SOVEREIGNTY IN EVERY DIMENSION.” Exit to real Gate, research, publication and music destinations.

## Production/look
- **Look:** smoky blacks, red mineral pigments, burnished brass/gold, etched concrete, blue dusk, archival grain and physical scale. No generic neon UI-only montage.
- **Human:** founder/John B for central protagonist; other Fragments with distinct identities. Never synthesize a generic lookalike and call it John Brajer.
- **Sound:** original composer/John B catalog where licensed, heartbeat, analog tape, architectural rumble, metallic door, resonant vocal moments.
- **Cameras:** macro detail → locked-down mid shots → wide architectural reveal → orbital conceptual scale. Minimal explanatory text, action first.
- **Aspect:** 16:9 master; safe-center framing for 9:16 teaser.
- **Accessible:** subtitle master and transcript, short alternative descriptions where feasible.
