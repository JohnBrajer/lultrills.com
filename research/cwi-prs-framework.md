# CWI and PRS

Status: Draft publication package
Author: John Brajer
Date: 2026-09-12

## CWI

CWI models a recurring constraint on human action: people often exercise free will inside a socially and psychologically permitted behavioral corridor composed of actions that feel familiar, legible, and survivable.

An unconventional action begins with high uncertainty and high differentiation. When someone visibly succeeds, the action becomes more conceivable to others. Repeated successes and failures update perceived risk and widen or contract the recognized action space. As social proof accumulates, certainty rises while novelty and status payoff generally fall, pushing the frontier of exceptional action outward again.

The key implication is that practical possibility is not exhausted by the actions physically available to a person. What matters is also the subset of actions a person psychologically permits themselves to consider.

This creates an early-mover asymmetry: early actors face greater uncertainty but can capture greater differentiation; later actors receive more evidence but usually less differentiation.

## PRS

PRS is a CWI sub-framework and decision lens built from three variables:

- Possibility — Can a human even do this?
- Risk perception — What happens to people who try?
- Status payoff — If I succeed, how extraordinary will society consider it?

PRS explains how unconventional actions move from unimaginable to conceivable to common. Observed success raises perceived possibility. Observed failure updates risk perception. Increasing adoption reduces novelty and status payoff.

PRS is therefore nested under CWI rather than functioning as a separate replacement framework.

## Mechanism summary

1. A candidate action lies outside the familiar behavioral corridor.
2. The actor evaluates whether it is psychologically conceivable, socially survivable, and worth the uncertainty.
3. Public outcomes feed back into collective belief about possibility and risk.
4. Repetition normalizes the action.
5. Normalization expands the recognized action space while reducing differentiation.
6. The frontier moves outward again.

## Applications

The framework is useful for analyzing entrepreneurship, creative careers, cultural innovation, social imitation, prestige dynamics, risk-taking, and any domain where actions become more thinkable after someone else demonstrates them.

## Canon relationship

CWI is the parent framework. PRS is a nested decision lens. The acronym CWI is intentionally left unexpanded until John Brajer formally defines its expansion.
